## Limits

This verifies persistence of the setting through a cold screen/app reopen in the emulator. The banner was deliberately suppressed and the setter/getter were instrumented. **It does not verify that incoming WhatsApp voice/video calls are actually blocked, that Premium is legitimately entitled, or that the feature is unlocked.** Candidate 264207409 is diagnostic-only and is not approved as a user update.

A first dispatch, run `41220244459`, stopped before starting the emulator because the archive contained `app.apk` and the workflow tried to move `app.apk` onto itself. This was a test-harness packaging bug, not an APK failure; fixed in commit `981d5ae`, after which run `41220312147` completed.
